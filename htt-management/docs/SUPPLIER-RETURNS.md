# Physical supplier returns

Entry: Purchases → Physical supplier returns, the link on a stock supplier bill, or `/purchases/supplier-returns`. The linked supplier credit opens the corresponding return for reversal.

This workflow posts a confirmed supplier credit and physical stock return together in the trusted local business engine. Select active bill/receipt matches, enter quantities in the original bill unit, supplier credit reference, date and reason. It uses the original receiving site, caps quantities by remaining matched receipts, checks stock, whole-pack rules, dates, open periods and original uncredited bill-line amounts. Duplicate lines, credit references and changed requests sharing an operation ID are rejected. A retry returns its original result without moving stock twice.

The credit uses the original bill price and tax snapshots: Dr AP / Cr input tax / Cr purchase price variance. Physical dispatch uses the site's moving weighted average: Dr purchase price variance / Cr inventory. Together they reverse the returned goods' inventory value, preserve tax cents and leave the difference between original bill value and current stock cost in variance. The last full-price partial credit uses remaining original cents. A price-only supplier credit continues to affect finances only. Receipt/bill matching and purchase-order received/billed history are retained; a return does not automatically order replacement goods or reopen GRNI.

A return's credit can be allocated against a supplier bill or refunded through the existing settlement workflow. Reverse those transactions before reversing the physical return. The linked credit cannot be voided independently. A source bill cannot be voided while source credits remain active, including unused credits. Return reversal restores the exact stock quantity and historical inventory issue value at the selected reversal date and reverses both original journals. It preserves the original return, credit, stock movements, costing movements and actors. Dates before subsequent inventory activity or credit settlements are rejected.

Purchasing and finance administrators can post/reverse combined returns. Accountants and auditors can read return history. Warehouse users receive no return cost register and cannot issue supplier credit through this workflow. Internal linked-credit insertion/reversal helpers are not IPC commands. Main-owned audit, period controls and atomic snapshot/storage commit and rollback cover the complete operation. No external supplier message, payment transfer or backend is connected.

Schema 9 adds the return register. Existing schema 1–8 data/backups migrate with empty return history and keep a pre-migration snapshot. Restore verifies matched source/credit quantities, stock/cost/journal/reversal links, costs, operation IDs and orphan return-credit references.

Automated acceptance covers confirmed returns and original tax cents, moving-average variance, multi-receipt/multi-site lines, conversions/whole packs, repeated/stale requests, over-return/over-credit, insufficient stock, direct void bypass, source-bill protection, allocations/refunds, failed posting/reversal rollback, historical AP/inventory reconciliation, closed periods, roles, restart, corrupt backups and schema-8 upgrade.

Boundary: this is a combined physical return with a newly confirmed full-source-price supplier credit. Uncredited warehouse shipments, linking a previously issued price credit, negotiated deductions and automatic replacement purchasing remain separate workflows. Expense/service bills and imported opening balances do not represent received physical stock and are excluded. The generic file attachment and external delivery workflows remain separate.

Native Electron acceptance used disposable synthetic data only: a stock bill for 10 base units (receipt cost 10.00, bill cost 12.00) was selected from Purchases; returning 2 units reduced the matched remainder from 10 to 8, recorded inventory value 20.00 and issued a linked credit for 24.00 net + 2.40 tax. The credit detail exposed the linked-return reversal action, and reversal restored the original stock and valuation while retaining VOID history and actor/reason evidence.
