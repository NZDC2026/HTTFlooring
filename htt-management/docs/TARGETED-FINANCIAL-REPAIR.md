# Targeted financial exception repair

G03 adds reviewed compound source metadata repair beyond bank exceptions: Sales/AP/credit summary correction, uniquely proven missing Sales result bindings and completed supplier-bill posting metadata. It uses independent approval, stale-evidence rejection, full rollback/restart proof and retained proposal history under schema 24. See [G03 source repair](G03-SOURCE-REPAIR.md) for exact eligibility, desktop acceptance and deliberate blockers. The bank procedures below remain available; they do not authorize arbitrary source reconstruction.

## Existing bank procedures

The financial recovery workbench now includes an executable main-process repair preview, durable proposal, independent approval, execution and post-repair verification. This phase supports BANK_TRANSACTION and BANK_TRANSFER under existing accounting.recover authority. No independent backend, payment service or broad system-journal edit command is added.

## Supported isolated defects

- CANCEL_NO_EFFECT: a valid saved bank intent has a durable bound result identity, but neither its business result nor source GL exists and no other repository/operation claims it. Current financial consistency must pass. A reviewer compares independent business/bank evidence; local absence does not establish whether an external transfer happened. Approval retires the local intent without creating any financial effect. Its original ID cannot be reused. The terminal operation retains resolution CANCELLED_NO_EFFECT and is displayed as Cancelled, not as a successful payment.
- RESTORE_BANK_RECORD: the bank result is missing while exactly one original POSTED source journal matches the saved request, date, reference, accounts, direction and cents. A prospective bank record must pass the existing bank closure proof and global financial audit. Execution restores that record with an explicit REC- recovery number and original bound identity. It preserves the source journal and all financial movements. Historical closed/reconciled records may be restored because no GL or clearing entry changes.
- RESTORE_BANK_JOURNAL: a unique bank result matches its saved intent, but its linked journal and every source journal are absent. No other repository may refer to the missing journal identity. The original date must pass open-period and bank reconciliation locks; bank/GL accounts must be active. Execution posts exactly the previewed source-owned debit/credit pair on the original date, updates this record's journal link and retains the missing prior identity in the repair plan. No period/date is silently substituted and no funds are transmitted.

- RESTORE_BANK_JOURNAL_LINK: a unique bank result and exactly one correct POSTED source journal match the saved intent, but the bank record points to a different journal ID. Projecting only the link change must pass full source proof and global audit. A nonexistent former ID must have no foreign references. A real unrelated journal and its rightful references remain intact. Independent approval updates only the bank record and corresponding financial cache link; the former/target IDs remain in the saved plan. Closed or reconciled dates are allowed because no journal, balance, match or clearing record changes.

## Review and integrity

Preview is read-only and available to audit.read. A proposal requires accounting.recover, a reference/reason and explicit checks of business records, GL/matches and independent evidence. Actor identities come from the verified main session; supplied actor/action/record/journal payloads are not accepted. A canonical SHA-256 digest covers current business snapshots and operation/review evidence. Proposal metadata is excluded from its own digest. Any source, account, period, journal or review change invalidates approval. The approver must differ from every recorded reviewer.

Approval reruns eligibility synchronously, executes only the stored plan's action, and verifies live source links/account movements plus global financial consistency. Metadata/result changes use the existing financial unit of work and main business-host rollback. A failure after posting, relinking or recording the outcome restores all command changes. An uncertain durable commit blocks further commands until restart. Identical acknowledged execution retries return their saved result; another approval cannot replay it.

Schema 23 signals the extended durable operation-log contract. Earlier snapshots retain their existing records and unresolved intents. Existing terminal state COMPLETED means the intent has been resolved; its resolution field distinguishes no-effect cancellation from repaired financial results. No fabricated result is inserted to make cancellation look like successful posting. Proposals, original request/result identity, plan/digest, trusted reviewer/approver, references, timestamps, post-repair digest and resulting journal identity survive restart and backup. Backup parsing validates proposal structure and bindings without registering business repositories.

## Cases deliberately blocked

Unbound intents, unreadable requests, nonidentical or ambiguous source journals, inconsistent financial cache/source copies, foreign ownership, reversed effects, unrelated accounting inconsistencies, unsupported Sales/purchase/reconciliation repair kinds, missing-journal repairs through closed/reconciled dates and missing journal IDs still used by matches/clearing remain blocked. Existing verified closure applies to already-complete results. No duplicate journal is deleted, no balancing entry hides an inconsistency, and no arbitrary record or posting date can be supplied. Multiple financial defects that cannot pass an isolated prospective audit still require a verified backup or a separate reviewed repair procedure.

## Exact bank duplicate correction

REVERSE_BANK_DUPLICATE supports BANK_TRANSACTION and BANK_TRANSFER only when exactly one business record already links to the correct canonical journal and exactly one extra original POSTED journal has identical source, original date, description/reference and two-account debit/credit amounts. Both journals must exactly match the saved intent and bank mappings. Neither journal can already be a reversal or correction. Duplicate IDs, extra source journals, an absent/ambiguous bank link, foreign references to the duplicate, unrelated financial inconsistencies and inactive bank/GL accounts block the plan. The original date must be open and after the bank reconciliation cutoff; no later posting date is substituted.

The main process derives the canonical/duplicate identities; operators cannot supply a selected journal or posting date. Existing independent proposal/approval, evidence digests, execution idempotency and rollback apply. Approval preserves the canonical journal and bank business record, retains every duplicate line, marks only the duplicate as REVERSED, and posts the exact opposite on the same date. A typed correction marker binds it to the canonical journal and repair operation. The durable outcome is BANK_DUPLICATE_REVERSED and records the resulting reversal ID.

A shared pure verifier recognizes only a fully linked, exact same-date zero-net pair against an unchanged POSTED canonical entry. Financial audits and bank closure proofs exclude only this proven duplicate from source cardinality; they continue checking all original and reversal amounts and lifecycle evidence. Invalid markers/pairs never suppress duplicate evidence. The normal source posting lookup resolves to the canonical entry. Startup/backup validation requires the same journal/cache contents, an exact pair and matching executed approval/plan in the operation log.

Both correction sides remain visible in GL and bank registers, labelled as internal duplicate offsets. They cannot be selected as imported-statement matches or bank reconciliation clearing. They do not inflate dashboard uncleared counts, outstanding payments/deposits or cash movement report totals; the cash report warns about their retained bank-register audit evidence. Net GL cash and report closing balances reflect the single intended transaction. A subsequent bank reconciliation can complete with the genuine entries alone.

This is a local duplicate-posting correction, not proof that an external bank transfer occurred only once. Reviewers must still compare independent bank evidence. Nonidentical duplicates, more than two source originals, unbound intents, mismatched amounts/accounts/dates, an ambiguous canonical link and closed/reconciled posting dates remain blocked for a separate reviewed recovery procedure.
