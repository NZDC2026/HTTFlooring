# Original-date bank categorisation corrections

A POSTED imported-statement classification can only be cancelled on its original statement date. The date must still pass existing accounting-period and bank reconciliation locks. Cancelling on a later date and reposting on the original date would count the original cash twice between those dates; this is now rejected before any journal or source mutation. MATCHED-line unlinking remains a metadata operation under its existing rules and is not forced to use the classification correction date.

The workbench selects the original date for POSTED rows, displays it read-only for the correction and explains that this changes categorisation rather than reversing an external bank payment. The main command independently enforces the date, permissions and source locks. After cancellation, the real imported movement remains UNMATCHED until its corrected classification or genuine existing source is chosen.

A pure proof derives internal cancellation pairs from existing BANK_STATEMENT ownership, generation identity, original/reversal links, status/timestamps, reference, complete account/amount/description lines, balanced whole-cent totals and identical financial dates. It supports multi-line tax-inclusive splits. Both original and reversing journals remain visible in GL and bank registers; no source record or journal is deleted and no new persisted marker or schema version is added.

Only exact same-date pairs are labelled internal offsets. They cannot be selected for imported matching or manual clearing, and do not inflate dashboard uncleared counts, outstanding deposits/payments or cash movement report totals. Report closing balances still reconcile to the complete GL/register. The newly classified movement remains the genuine bank activity, with its current account and tax treatment. A subsequent reconciliation can complete with those genuine rows alone.

Existing valid same-date histories remain compatible. Earlier clients could leave the exact cancellation pair in equal gross outstanding deposits/payments, or clear both sides in the same saved statement. The shared proof accepts only the precisely recalculated legacy totals and/or both members of an exact same-date pair in that same statement. It does not accept a single offset, split clearing across statements, arbitrary equal outstanding amounts or external matches to internal rows. Old totals and clearing IDs remain unchanged; new statements use normalized internal-offset treatment.

Legacy cross-date, orphaned or malformed categorisation reversals remain visible and are labelled for review. Their amounts/dates are retained in cash reports with a warning; they are not silently excluded or moved to another date. Their offset rows cannot be matched/cleared as external movements. Reclassification/reversal of their owning line and bank reconciliation/close verification remain blocked for reviewed recovery. A validated backup or a separately approved accounting repair is required; this patch does not automatically rewrite historical journals or completed statements. Both POSTED and UNMATCHED legacy line states are covered.

Regression coverage includes rejection without mutation, same-date multi-line cancellation, changed expense account and tax code, daily/window cash balance equivalence, restored GST treatment, dashboard counts, visible retained originals, candidate/clearing protection, successful next reconciliation and period preflight, closed-period/completed-statement locks, post-reversal rollback and retry, source/amount/date/lifecycle corruption, orphan offsets and legacy cross-date recovery blockers.
